Our story

Daniel’s swan plants was founded in September 2022, by Daniel Saunders with a vision to provide people with swan plants to feed their hungry caterpillars. What first started with 15 plants has expanded to over 500 swan plants and spread across 3 greenhouses. We have helped many people feed their hungry caterpillars and provide plants to attract monarch butterflies to their garden.
